A little context on coverage

This searches Google Lens results through SerpApi. It does not search every social-media platform or the entire internet. Private, unindexed, or inaccessible content may never appear. Results are provider suggestions; no similarity scores are invented. “Provider exact matches” reflects the response’s exact-matches tab, not an independent verification.

New file searches upload your image to SerpApi; URL searches send its public address. Thumbnails are fetched from their hosts through Python when enabled. No tracking scripts or telemetry are included.
